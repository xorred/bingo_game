How to play with a friend
  1. Send your friend the link to this page.
  2. Create a room and send them the 4-letter code.
  3. Both of you shuffle your grids if you like. Once the first number is called, grids lock.
  4. Take turns tapping a number on your own grid. It gets crossed on everyone's grid.
  5. Every full row, column or diagonal cuts one letter of B-I-N-G-O. First to cut all five wins.
